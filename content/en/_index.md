## About Me

**Senior Game Engineer — Gameplay, Multiplayer, Unity, Unreal, C++**

I am a game engineer with over **15 years of professional experience** building commercial games for **PC, mobile, Web, and console**. My work spans action games, ARPGs, CCGs, simulation games, and live-service titles, from early architecture and prototyping through launch and long-term operation.

I specialize in **gameplay and combat systems, multiplayer networking, game architecture, AI, development tools, and production pipelines**. I work primarily with **C++, C#, Python, Unity, Unreal Engine, and proprietary C++ engines**.

I have built multiple projects from the ground up and led engineering teams of up to six developers, including architecture and code review, mentoring, hiring, planning, and close collaboration with design, art, production, and server teams.

I also care deeply about how games *feel*, not only how they are implemented. I have spent thousands of hours with games such as *Street Fighter V*, *Elden Ring*, *Cyberpunk 2077*, and *Baldur's Gate 3*, and I bring that player perspective into gameplay and engineering decisions.

- **Engines & Languages:** Unity, Unreal Engine 4/5, proprietary C++ engines | C++, C#, Python, Lua
